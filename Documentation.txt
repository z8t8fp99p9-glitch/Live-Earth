LIVE // EARTH — Aqua MODIS coordinate precision repair

This patch keeps the NASA Aqua MODIS Level-3 daily 4 km chlorophyll product
(erdMH1chla1day_R2022NRT). It does not use DINEOF, gap-filling, interpolation,
or a replacement sensor/product. NOAA OISST remains on the Upwell mirror.

The latest Actions run showed that the MODIS download and NetCDF decode were
reached, but the decoder rejected longitude coordinates because it compared
CSV and NetCDF coordinate serialization at 1e-6 degrees. The repair reads the
coordinates embedded in the returned NetCDF, validates count, monotonicity,
regular 0.125-degree sampled spacing, and positional agreement within 0.00005
degrees (well below one native MODIS cell), then passes those exact returned
coordinates to the existing decoder. A one-cell shift still fails validation.

The output remains exactly 2880 x 1440, sampled every third native MODIS cell.
No interpolation or synthetic values are introduced.

Commit title: Validate Aqua MODIS NetCDF Coordinate Precision
