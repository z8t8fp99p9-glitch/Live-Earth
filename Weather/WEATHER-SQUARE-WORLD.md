## Traditional entrance stair refinement
- Replace the four thin, separated plank treads with four traditional solid timber risers.
- Give each riser a shallow stepped rise and a deeper body, with a broad timber cap that slightly overhangs the riser.
- Keep the stair width centred on the pavilion entrance and preserve the existing four-step elevation progression.
- Use the existing timber and deck materials so the stairs read as part of the older Japanese wooden structure, rather than a modern floating staircase.
