# Clear Weather Drawing Plan — A Clear Day at the Maple Pavilion

**Plan ID:** `clear-maple-pavilion-sequence-v1`  
**Artwork ID:** `clear-maple-pavilion-001`  
**Source of truth:** `Weather/Art/References/clear-weather-master-001.jpg`  
**Status:** Approved master; ordered-path representation prepared.

## Composition to preserve

Reproduce the supplied master as-is: large maple trunk and branch entering from the left; red maple leaves; the small pavilion roof and support beneath the branch; hanging wind chime and paper charm near the centre; two distant birds; open vermilion sun and its delicate light texture at upper right; two quiet mountain ridges and sparse horizon/water marks; curved foreground bank with stones, grasses and a small flowering twig. Maintain the original asymmetry, negative space, object scale, and placement.

## Replay stages

| Order | Stage | Weight / treatment | Required content |
|---:|---|---|---|
| 1 | Maple tree structure | Thick structural near-black | Main trunk, taper, connected branches, original brush edges |
| 2 | Pavilion structure | Thick to medium near-black | Roof lines, central support, railings |
| 3 | Distant mountains and horizon | Medium/thin; limited soft grey | Mountain ridges, horizon/cloud/water marks |
| 4 | Foreground garden and water edge | Medium/thin near-black | Bank, stream marks, stones, grasses, flowering twig |
| 5 | Wind chime and birds | Fine to medium near-black | Hanging bell, open paper charm, two birds |
| 6 | Vermilion autumn accents | Source red tones and paper highlights | Red sun, maple leaves, drifting leaves, small blossoms and charm accent |

The path array must follow this order. The renderer has no named stage pauses; path order alone controls progressive Replay.

## Exclusions

No new objects, no redesign, no cropping, no stretching, no altered palette, no text, no border, no added decoration, no automatic weather/day/night recolouring, and no changes to Rain or the shared renderer.


## Fidelity note

The master JPEG is retained unchanged. The ordered-path asset reproduces the master's composition and extracted ink/red contours in the renderer's supported format. The current renderer draws closed vector contours and its own warm-paper ground; it cannot reproduce every individual JPEG paper-grain pixel or the full continuous raster texture byte-for-byte. Do not describe the path rendering as pixel-identical. If preserving every raster texture mark becomes a requirement, stop and propose a separate, explicitly approved renderer capability rather than redesigning or silently simplifying this artwork.
