# LIVE // EARTH — Live Art Production Standard Addendum 2.4

**Status:** Binding master-wide standard for all new Live Art  
**Effective:** 13 November 2026  
**Supersedes:** Prior line-art-first direction and the 75-second / 63-gesture Clear 002 replay approach for future artwork. Historical assets and documents remain preserved.

## Non-negotiable standard

- Retain the approved painted artwork and its professional brush character. Never replace it with line art or a simplified redraw.
- Maximum 100 continuous brush gestures for the whole artwork.
- Exactly 30 seconds from first brush mark to completion.
- Follow this order: thick structural brushwork; attached thin brushwork; air, birds and little details; leaves; sun last; finishing gesture.
- The original master supplies the final paint, brush texture, colour and paper. Animated strokes reveal the master; they do not redraw it.
- Use fewer than 100 strokes when fidelity allows. Never add meaningless marks to fill the quota.

## Replay contract

The authored ordered array is the replay order. Every stroke has a coherent centreline, pressure profile, base width and natural taper. Five ordered stages represent the required sequence. Stage timing must fit inside 30,000 milliseconds total, including pauses. No later stage may start before the earlier stage is complete.

## Master reveal contract

The app keeps the exact approved raster master below a warm-paper veil. Brush gestures erase the veil in sequence. Once all gestures complete, the entire master is visible. This ensures the final scene retains its original painted contours, pigment texture, tiny details and expressive shapes, without forcing raster art through a vector line-art approximation.
