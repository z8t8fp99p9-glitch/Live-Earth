# Weather Live Art — Artwork Drawing Plans

This document records the artwork-specific direction. The global style is defined in `LIVE-ART-PRODUCTION-GUIDE.md` and `ART-DIRECTION.json`. A drawing plan never overrides the global 10:7, 70/30, monochrome, stroke hierarchy, or Replay requirements.

## A Rainy Tea Afternoon (`rainy-tea-afternoon-001`)

**Status:** Approved sequence.  
**Moment:** A quiet cup beside a rain-streaked window, with hills and a small pavilion beyond.

1. **The door / window frame** — strong, confident thick strokes establish the setting and sill.
2. **The tree branch** — flowing thick branch strokes, then a few medium-weight leaves.
3. **The hills** — long, elegant, restrained medium-to-thin lines in the distance.
4. **The little house / pavilion** — medium strokes; only the essential roof and building outlines.
5. **The kettle and teacup** — carefully drawn medium outlines and very few details; interiors remain mostly warm paper.
6. **The red essence** — red sun, selected red maple leaves, and intended tiny accents appear last.

**Replay contract:** the asset's `paths` records must be authored in this sequence. The current renderer progressively reveals the array in stored order. It does not pause at named stage boundaries.
