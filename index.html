Data & privacy

◇

This prototype has no account, advertising, analytics, or app-owned server. Weather and earthquake requests go directly to their public providers. Browser location is requested only after you tap the location button.

Live data requires internet access. The app shell can be cached for offline opening after the first successful visit; cached information must not be mistaken for live information.

Prototype note: the globe is illustrative, Sun/Moon calculations are approximate, and traffic is not yet connected. Free provider quotas and terms must be checked before claiming worldwide live coverage.
