# Initial October 2026 Nature seed

The Collector includes the eight source-linked editorial summaries already present in the project's October 2026 Nature monthly research fixture. The summaries are paraphrased digests, not original article text. This preserves the October work for the first publication when RSS feeds may already have rolled forward.

The seed imports once, and only during November 2026, into Nature's own database. The Collector records the import marker in `publisher_meta`. Normal daily RSS collection remains the continuing source path. RSS items may refresh seeded metadata when encountered.

The seed contains no photographs. The six documentary candidates are verified independently from their original Wikimedia Commons file pages on every run. The giant-water-lily story intentionally has no image because the previously reviewed *Victoria amazonica* image is a different species from the reported *Victoria colombiana*.

The eight summaries and their source URLs were already part of the project's test fixture. They are not full article text and must not be represented as such.
